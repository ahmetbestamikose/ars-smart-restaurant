# Maven Analytics – Restaurant Orders

A quarter's worth of orders (1 January – 31 March 2023) from a fictitious restaurant serving
international cuisine. 32 menu items, 12 234 order lines, 5 370 order IDs (5 343 after removing the
137 lines without an item).

- Source: Maven Analytics, Data Playground, "Restaurant Orders"
  https://mavenanalytics.io/data-playground/restaurant-orders
- Licence stated by the source: GPL-3.0 (https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-3.0.html)
- Files are redistributed unchanged: `menu_items.csv`, `order_details.csv`,
  `restaurant_db_data_dictionary.csv`.
